Campaign Performance: Spend, CAC & ROAS by Channel

Question: Which channels drive efficient growth, and where should the next £1,000 go?

1. Data

Sample dataset: marketing_campaign.csv (Kaggle / HubSpot sample). Columns: date, channel (Paid Search, Paid Social, Email, Organic), spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue. Cleaned in Sheets: removed duplicates, standardised channel names, added week column.

2. Analysis

Email: 6.1xBest ROAS

SQL used for aggregation (example — paste your real query):

SELECT
  channel,
  SUM(spend) AS spend,
  SUM(conversions) AS conv,
  SUM(revenue) AS revenue,
  ROUND(SUM(spend)/NULLIF(SUM(conversions),0),2) AS cac,
  ROUND(SUM(revenue)/NULLIF(SUM(spend),0),2) AS roas
FROM marketing_campaign
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY roas DESC;
